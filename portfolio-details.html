Stack

Hardware: Arduino, PCA9685, servos, ultrasonic & IR sensors.

Software: Python, OpenCV, Arduino (C++), serial comms (I2C, UART).

About the Project

Building my InMoov humanoid robot started as a personal tribute to my father and grew into a full R&D journey. I designed, printed and assembled structural parts, wired high‑torque servos, and integrated sensors, then brought everything to life with Python and Arduino.

Build Process

  • 3D printing and mechanical assembly of 30+ components.
  • Servo calibration, power management and cable routing.
  • Embedded control via Arduino; high‑level logic in Python.

Skills Gained

  • Electronics: circuit design, soldering, safe power distribution.
  • Mechanics: linkage design, gear ratios, motion planning.
  • Software: OpenCV for vision, real‑time control loops, serial protocols.
  • Systems integration across Linux (Raspberry Pi) and Arduino.

Why it Matters

Beyond the technical challenge, this build represents patience, curiosity and the belief that ambitious ideas are achievable. Sharing this work helps others learn, experiment and build their own.
