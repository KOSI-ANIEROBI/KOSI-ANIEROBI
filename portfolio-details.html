🤖 InMoov Robot Project – A Tribute to My Father

Building my own InMoov humanoid robot was more than just a technical achievement it was a deeply personal project, crafted with love, curiosity, and a vision to create something meaningful for my father, who has always been my inspiration. Every wire, sensor, and printed part carries a story one of perseverance, learning, and innovation.

🛠️ The Build Process

This project began with the dream of constructing a life-sized, open-source 3D-printed robot capable of human-like gestures and interaction. I started by carefully printing and assembling the robot’s structural components, using precision to connect the joints, servos, and frames. The mechanical structure was brought to life through a combination of: 3D printing over 30+ parts High-torque servo motors for articulation Custom wiring and soldering for robust power delivery The process demanded an intricate understanding of biomechanics, electronics, and robotic systems — and I eagerly embraced the challenge.

🔧 Skills Gained

Throughout the journey, I developed and refined a powerful set of interdisciplinary skills: 🧩 Electronics & Hardware: Circuit design and soldering Power management for servo control Sensor integration (ultrasonic, IR, mic) 🛠️ Mechanical Engineering: CAD design and 3D printing Gear ratio calibration Joint and linkage design 💻 Software & Programming: Python scripting for logic and AI Arduino (C++) for real-time control Serial communication protocols (I2C, UART) Basic computer vision using OpenCV 🌐 System Integration: Bridging software and hardware into one intelligent, responsive system Ensuring cross-platform stability between Linux (Raspberry Pi) and Arduino firmware

❤️ Why I Built It
I built this robot for my dad to show him that the impossible is possible with passion, patience, and purpose. It is a gift, a tribute, and a symbol of the future we're building. Watching the robot come to life in his presence is a moment I will never forget.
